The company was founded by successful restaurateur and global food explorer, Craig Lieberman, after he discovered a uniquely thin cracker while traveling in Australia., Made with only a handful of premium quality, simple ingredients, the company offers a line of versatile Entertaining Crisps in nine flavors, four sweet snackable Sweet Crisps flavors and three bold new Snaps snacking flavors including Sea Salt, Everything and Umami., Quality and Sustainability Our bakery is not only the source of our delicious crisps, it is a source of pride., To ensure the highest levels of quality assurance and food safety, our bakery is gold certified and regularly audited by Silliker, the leading internationally accredited food testing and consulting network., Community We believe that everyone should have access to good food and pledge to share our crisps with friends and neighbors in need., Philanthropic efforts also include a quarterly donation of more than 300,000 crisps to Food Bank of the Rockies Totes of Hope program, where to-go totes are filled with nutritious kid-friendly foods to help sustain children over the weekend when school is not in session.

Management practices

Certifications Certified production practices are those mandated by certification standards and enforced by 3rd party certification verifiers. Violating a practice removes a company's eligibility for the certification. Thus, a company is guaranteed to follow them if they received the corresponding certification (assuming no corruption). These practices are distinguished from production practices implied by the certification’s terminology (i.e. “organic”), but aren’t formally enforced. For example, poor soil management on an organic certified farm.
