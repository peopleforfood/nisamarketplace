Management practices

Certifications Certified production practices are those mandated by certification standards and enforced by 3rd party certification verifiers. Violating a practice removes a company's eligibility for the certification. Thus, a company is guaranteed to follow them if they received the corresponding certification (assuming no corruption). These practices are distinguished from production practices implied by the certification’s terminology (i.e. “organic”), but aren’t formally enforced. For example, poor soil management on an organic certified farm.
