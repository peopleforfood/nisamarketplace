Founding Story & Company Mission
Founding Story
It was over 25 years ago that Organic Meadow pioneered the organic dairy movement in Canada. We brought together like-minded farmers that cared about creating dairy products using nature as their driving force. We continue that same mission today by rolling up our sleeves and crafting wonderful organic dairy products just like we did our first day on the farm.
Company Mission
We set ourselves a high bar to use old world methods and well-treated animals to craft fresh, organic dairy products. We may have added a few new products and flavours, but our main foundation will always remain: create exceptional organic dairy products using the best of nature and some very hard-working people to keep it that way.

    Certified production practices

    Certified production practices are those mandated by certification standards and enforced by 3rd party certification verifiers. Violating a practice removes a company's eligibility for the certification. Thus, a company is guaranteed to follow them if they received the corresponding certification (assuming no corruption). These practices are distinguished from production practices implied by the certification’s terminology (i.e. “organic”), but aren’t formally enforced.
